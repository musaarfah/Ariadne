# Pre-registration — expanded role scope

**Committed before the expanded roles are fitted or evaluated.**

---

## Why this needs pre-registering at all

The six-role model produced an **inconclusive** gate: crew 0.730 against director-only 0.680, 95% CI
[−0.020, +0.100]. Expanding role scope afterwards is exactly the kind of flexibility that manufactures
results — try more roles until one clears the bar, then report that one.

So the role list is fixed here, **before anything is fitted**, and it is chosen on a single criterion
that has nothing to do with whether it helps:

> **Does this person make decisions that shape what the finished film is like?**

Craft authorship, not headcount and not business. A role is included or excluded by that question alone.
The counts below were visible when this list was written — that is unavoidable, since the point was to
find roles with enough data to measure — but they were **not** the basis for inclusion, and the
exclusions below prove it: the single largest pool available is deliberately rejected.

## Included

| department / job | people with ≥12 films | why it is authorial |
|---|---|---|
| Production / Casting | 24 | Casting directors shape performance and ensemble; a recognised creative credit |
| Sound / Playback Singer | 19 | In Indian cinema the singer is a primary creative presence, not a technician. 308 of these 1,297 films are Indian |
| Sound / Supervising Sound Editor | 14 | Leads the sound edit; the authorial role in a soundtrack's construction |
| Sound / Sound Designer | 9 | Designs the film's sonic identity |
| Costume & Make-Up / Costume Design | 7 | Designs how everyone on screen looks |
| Crew / Choreographer | 7 | Authors entire sequences, especially in the Indian films that are 24% of this library |

Six added roles, **80 people** with ≥12 films, against 34 in the existing six.

## Excluded, and why

| department / job | ≥12 films | why excluded |
|---|---|---|
| **Crew / Stunts** | **86** | **The largest pool available and rejected outright.** 4,931 individuals — this is headcount, not authorship. A stunt performer executes; they do not decide what the film is like |
| Sound / Sound Re-Recording Mixer | 33 | Technical execution of a mix specified by others |
| Sound / Foley Artist, Foley Mixer, Foley | 20/10/6 | Craft, but reproducing sound rather than deciding it |
| Production / Executive Producer | 15 | Financing and business, not craft |
| Production / Producer | 12 | Organisational; too heterogeneous to read as authorship |
| Sound / Musician | 12 | Performs a composer's score |
| Sound / Sound Effects Editor, Dialogue Editor, ADR Mixer | 11/6/10 | Execution under the supervising sound editor, who is included |
| Camera / Still Photographer | 6 | Produces publicity images, not the film |
| Crew / Stunt Coordinator | 5 | Borderline — designs sequences, but excluded for consistency with Stunts. Revisit separately if ever |
| Writing / Characters | 5 | Source-material credit, same reasoning as excluding `Novel` (D61) |
| Production / ADR Voice Casting | 5 | Narrow subset of casting, already covered |
| Camera / Aerial Director of Photography | 5 | Second-unit specialist, not the film's cinematographer |

## Predictions, recorded now

1. **The gate stays inconclusive.** Adding roles does not add test films, so the interval will not shrink
   enough to exclude zero. Confidence: high.
2. **The share of effects surviving attribution rises above the current 13/31.** Editors are unusually
   loyal to one director, which is why Aarti Bajaj's effect was absorbed; casting directors, costume
   designers and sound designers move between directors more freely, so more of their effects should be
   separable. **This is the main reason to expect the expansion to be worth anything.**
3. **Playback Singer and Choreographer will show effects concentrated in Indian cinema**, and will
   therefore be the roles most exposed to the regional bias in the expectation model — which is why the
   expectation fix has to land first.
4. At least one added role will produce **no** effect beyond the permutation null.

## Rules

- Multiple-comparisons: twelve roles tested at p<0.05 expects ~0.6 false positives. Per-role
  permutation nulls stay as they are, and the writeup reports the number of roles tested alongside any
  significant finding.
- Every added role's coverage is reported next to its results, as D67 requires for production design.
- **No roles are added after this file is committed** without a dated amendment explaining why.
